Iamb mock-ups

Six flows, drawn screen by screen for the review of task 3.1. Each page shows the steps side by side with the keys the user presses, what the screen does and what the service is asked. Use the switches at the top right to see light and dark, and macOS and Windows window chrome.

The written specification is in spec.md, the tokens and components in design-system.md, and the sheet to fill in while reviewing in review.md.

FLOW 1

Onboarding

Install, grant permissions, save a first snippet and see it expand. Under 2 minutes, no help.

 FLOW 2

Create a snippet

The library, the editor with macros as chips, the live preview, the warnings before saving.

 FLOW 3

Expand

What happens in other apps, the menu-bar and tray menu, and “Why didn’t it expand?”.

 FLOW 4

Fill-in

The form for snippets with blanks: every field type, insert, cancel, the same form again.

 FLOW 5

Search

The palette behind one shortcut: find a snippet by a few letters and insert it.

 FLOW 6

Share a folder

Share with a group, receive a folder, and review snippets that can press keys.
